Cookie Policy

Effective: 26 August 2026 · Xenomere OÜ

In short: This website uses no cookies at all. The Xenomere app uses one strictly necessary session cookie. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising cookies.

1. Cookies on this website (xenomere.com)

This marketing and legal website does not use any cookies. There are no analytics scripts, no tracking pixels, no advertising tags, and no third-party cookies.

2. Cookies in the Xenomere app

The Xenomere application (iOS, Android, and web) uses a single cookie:

CookiePurposeDurationType
xenomere_session Maintains your authenticated session. HTTP-only, first-party, Secure flag set. Session (expires when you close the browser or sign out) Strictly necessary

This cookie is essential for the app to function. It does not track you across websites and is not used for advertising or analytics.

3. No analytics or tracking

We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, or any other analytics or tracking tool on this website or in the app. We do not use advertising identifiers.

4. Managing cookies

Since this website uses no cookies, there is nothing to manage here. For the app's session cookie:

  • Browser: You can clear cookies in your browser settings. This will sign you out of the web app.
  • iOS / Android: The app uses secure storage, not browser cookies. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes the session.

5. Changes

If we introduce additional cookies in the future, we will update this policy and, where required by law, obtain your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at contact@xenomere.com.
