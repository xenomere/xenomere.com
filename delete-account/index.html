Delete Your Xenomere Account

App: Xenomere · Developer: Xenomere OÜ

You can delete your account and personal data at any time. Choose the method that works best for you.

Option A — In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Scroll to Danger Zone and tap Delete Account.
  3. Read the warning and tap Delete Account again to confirm. This cannot be undone.
  4. This removes your account and the personal data we are not legally required to keep.

In-app deletion is immediate for application records.

Option B — By email (no app required)

If you have already uninstalled the app or prefer not to reinstall:

  1. Send an email to contact@xenomere.com from the same email address you registered with.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Xenomere account
  3. We will match the account, delete it within 30 days (usually faster), and email you when it is done.

You do not need to reinstall the app.

What we delete

  • Account record and profile
  • Wearable samples we stored
  • Lab results and marker values
  • Body-scan photos and derived estimates
  • Journal entries and dose/compound notes
  • Coach chat transcripts
  • Questionnaire responses (PHQ-9, GAD-7, etc.)
  • Findings and analysis records
  • Life events and n-of-1 experiment logs
  • Protocol and workout data
  • Waitlist identity (anonymised)

What we may be required to keep

  • Tax invoices: Estonian accounting law may require retention for up to 7 years.
  • Security logs: Limited retention for fraud prevention and legal claims.

What we cannot cancel for you

Apple or Google subscriptions keep billing until you cancel them in your store account settings:

  • Apple: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions
  • Google Play: Open Play Store → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

Wearable accounts (Oura, Garmin, etc.) stay yours — we only revoke our access token. We cannot delete your account with the wearable provider.

Important: Deleting the Xenomere app from your phone is not the same as deleting your account. Auto-renewing subscriptions continue until cancelled in Apple or Google settings.

Timeline

  • In-app deletion: Immediate for application records.
  • Email request: Within 30 days, usually faster. You will receive a confirmation email.

Questions

If you have questions about account deletion or data retention, contact us at contact@xenomere.com.
