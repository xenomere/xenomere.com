Wellness & Self-Tracking

Track training, recovery, and the numbers you already measure.

Xenomere is a personal wellness notebook. Log workouts and meals. Connect a wearable if you want. Keep lab results in one place. Look at trends over time.

Not a medical device. Not medical advice. Not a pharmacy. Xenomere does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.

App Store Google Play

Workout Programs

Log sessions, track load, and follow adaptive progression. Keep a simple training history with estimates — not a coaching licence.

Nutrition & Food Database

Log meals and calories with a real food database. Get a density score based on what you entered — an estimate, not a prescription.

Wearables & Apple Health

Pull overnight HRV, sleep, and resting heart rate from Apple Health, Health Connect, or a wearable you authorise — or type them in manually.

Lab Result Tracking

Photograph or type marker values from a panel you obtained elsewhere. We chart them over time. We do not order tests or act as a laboratory.

N-of-1 Experiments

Record what you changed and what you observed. These are your notes — not a clinical trial and not a prescription.

Guided Health Paths

Structured check-ins, trend analysis, and personalised insights based on your data. Decision-support estimates — not diagnosis.

Xenomere does not sell compounds, recommend a pharmacy, or calculate a medical dose for you. If you keep personal notes about substances, that is your record — not our supply chain.

Your Data

Your data stays under your account.

Export it anytime. Delete the account from the app or from xenomere.com/delete-account. We do not sell personal data and we do not use health data for advertising.
